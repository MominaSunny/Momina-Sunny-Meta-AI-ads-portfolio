Mobile App & Web Dashboard — Civic Engagement

CivicFix

A citizen-focused product that makes reporting and tracking local community issues simple, transparent and accountable — designed as a mobile app for citizens and a web dashboard for the people responsible for resolving issues.

Role
UI/UX Designer
Tools
Figma
Platform
Mobile App + Web Dashboard
Focus
End-to-end product design
Problem

Reporting a local issue rarely leads anywhere.

Broken streetlights, potholes and neglected public spaces often go unreported simply because there's no clear, low-effort way to flag them — and no visibility into whether anything happens afterward. CivicFix needed to close that loop for both sides: the citizen reporting the issue, and the authority resolving it.

Research & Understanding

Designing for two very different users.

The product needed to work for a citizen reporting an issue in seconds on the street, and for an administrator triaging dozens of reports at a desk. That meant two distinct interfaces, built on one shared data model — one fast and mobile-first, one dense and dashboard-oriented — while staying visually and structurally connected.

User Needs

What each side of the experience had to deliver.

01

Fast reportingA citizen should be able to flag an issue with a photo and a location in a few taps.

02

Visible progressPeople need to see that a report was received and is moving, not disappearing into a void.

03

Organized triageAdministrators need reports sorted and prioritized clearly, without digging through clutter.

04

Shared accountabilityBoth sides should see the same status language, so nothing gets lost in translation.

UX Framework

One accountable system for two connected audiences.

01

Wireframe overviewThe citizen flow stays focused on reporting, while the dashboard structure supports triage, ownership and resolution.

02

Information architecture and navigationIssue type, location and status form the shared information model across the mobile app and administrative dashboard.

03

UX strategy and design decisionsProgressive reporting keeps the first action quick, while status history gives people a clear reason to return.

04

Usability, responsive and accessible foundationsLarge touch targets, plain status language, strong contrast and responsive tables support use in the field and at a desk.

05

Design system and key UX improvementsShared status colors, labels and interaction patterns make handoffs clearer and turn an isolated complaint into a trackable service.

Design Direction

Clear, civic and quietly confident.

A restrained palette and status-driven color coding help both citizens and administrators read a report's state instantly, without relying on dense text.

Outcome

A shared, visible line between reporting and resolving.

CivicFix gives citizens a fast, low-friction way to speak up, and gives administrators a clear system to act on what they hear — turning a one-way complaint into a visible, trackable process.
