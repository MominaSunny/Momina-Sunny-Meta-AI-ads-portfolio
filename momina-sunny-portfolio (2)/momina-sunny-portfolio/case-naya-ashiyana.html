Mobile Application — Rental Marketplace

Naya Ashiyana

A premium rental marketplace app designed to make finding a trustworthy home feel effortless, transparent and refined.

Role
UI/UX Designer
Tools
Figma
Platform
Mobile Application
Focus
End-to-end product design
Problem

Renting a home rarely feels safe or straightforward.

Most rental listings online feel outdated, cluttered or untrustworthy — vague photos, unclear pricing and no real sense of who is on the other end. Naya Ashiyana set out to change that: a rental experience that feels considered and premium from the first screen, where every listing feels verified and every interaction feels intentional.

Research & Understanding

Getting close to how people actually search for a home.

Before any screen was drawn, the focus was on understanding the emotional weight behind renting — the hesitation, the comparison-shopping, the need for reassurance. Looking at existing rental platforms surfaced a common pattern: dense information with very little visual trust. That gap became the starting point for the design direction.

User Needs

What the experience had to deliver.

01

Clarity over clutterListings that surface the details people actually decide on — location, price, condition — without visual noise.

02

A sense of trustVisual cues and verified presentation that make a listing feel credible at a glance.

03

Effortless comparisonAn easy way to browse, save and compare options without losing context.

04

A premium feelAn interface that reflects the value of the decision being made — finding a home.

UX Framework

A rental experience organized around confidence.

01

Wireframe overviewThe structure moves from discovery to comparison, then to a focused property detail and enquiry path.

02

Information architecture and navigationSearch, saved listings and account actions stay close to the browsing journey, while filters remain easy to revisit.

03

UX strategy and design decisionsVerified details, clear pricing and progressive disclosure reduce uncertainty without overwhelming the first view.

04

Usability, responsive and accessible foundationsConsistent controls, readable contrast, predictable touch targets and flexible layouts support confident use across devices and abilities.

05

Design system and key UX improvementsA restrained type, spacing and status language system creates consistency while making comparison and enquiry feel faster and more deliberate.

Design Direction

A visual language built on trust and restraint.

The interface leans on generous imagery, calm typography and a warm neutral palette that lets each property feel like the focus — never competing with the interface around it.

Captured directly from the interactive Figma prototype — the actual browsing, filtering and listing flow as a renter would experience it.

Outcome

An app that makes a big decision feel lighter.

Naya Ashiyana turns the anxiety of house-hunting into a calm, guided experience — one where every listing feels considered and every step feels like progress, not friction.
