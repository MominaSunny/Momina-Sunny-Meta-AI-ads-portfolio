Mobile Application — AI Fashion Design

Suitora

An AI-powered fashion designing app that turns ideas into wearable concepts, blending creative freedom with an intuitive, guided interface.

Role
UI/UX Designer
Tools
Figma
Platform
Mobile Application
Focus
End-to-end product design
Problem

AI tools are powerful, but rarely feel designed for a fashion mind.

Most generative design tools speak in prompts and sliders — technical, not creative. Suitora needed to feel like a design studio, not a machine-learning console: a space where someone thinking in fabric, silhouette and mood could explore freely without fighting the interface.

Research & Understanding

Learning how designers actually think.

The starting point was understanding how a fashion designer's process moves — from mood and reference to sketch, iteration and refinement. That shaped an interface built around visual exploration first, with AI generation woven in as a creative accelerant rather than the entire experience.

UX Framework

A creative workflow that keeps the designer in control.

Wireframe overviewThe structure gives the visual canvas priority, with generation, refinement and saved concepts available without interrupting exploration.

02

03

UX strategy and design decisionsPlain-language controls turn complex AI actions into familiar creative choices and make iteration feel intentional.

Usability, responsive and accessible foundationsClear focus states, readable labels, generous touch targets and flexible panels support concentrated work on smaller screens.

05

Design system and key UX improvementsConsistent controls, restrained accent use and lightweight feedback reduce technical friction while preserving creative momentum.

Design Direction

Editorial, confident, quietly futuristic.

A dark, gallery-like canvas lets generated designs read like fashion photography, with a single accent color reserved for the moments that matter — generation, selection and saving.

Outcome

AI that feels like a creative partner, not a tool.

Suitora turns generative fashion design into something that feels intuitive and expressive — giving designers a space to explore ideas quickly, without ever feeling like they're operating a machine.
