Introduction — Momina Sunny

I design with people at the center, not just pixels on a screen.

My process starts with understanding real behavior — how people think, hesitate and decide — before a single screen gets drawn. From there, every wireframe, flow and interface decision is made to remove friction and bring clarity.

How I Work

Design Process

A consistent rhythm behind every project, adapted to what each problem actually needs.

Understand

Getting close to the problem and the people affected by it.

Research

Gathering context through conversations and comparable products.

Define

Framing the real problem worth solving.

Ideate

Exploring directions before committing to one.

Wireframe

Structuring flows and hierarchy before visual design.

Prototype

Bringing the interface to life as something clickable.

Test

Watching real people use it and noting where they pause.

Refine

Polishing detail, motion and consistency.
