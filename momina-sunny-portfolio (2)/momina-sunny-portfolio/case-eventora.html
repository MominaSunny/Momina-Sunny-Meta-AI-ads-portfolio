Website — Event Experience

Eventora

A modern event management website designed to make discovering, planning and hosting events feel effortless and engaging.

Role
UI/UX Designer
Tools
Figma
Platform
Website
Focus
End-to-end product design
Problem

Event platforms often bury the excitement in logistics.

Between ticketing, scheduling and venue details, most event websites feel more like admin tools than something people want to browse. Eventora needed to bring the energy of an event back to the forefront — while still making the practical side, finding and booking an event, effortless.

Research & Understanding

Balancing discovery with decision-making.

Looking at how people browse events revealed two mindsets happening at once — casual discovery ("what's happening this weekend") and intentional planning ("I need to book this specific event"). The design needed to serve both without forcing one experience onto the other.

UX Framework

A booking journey that protects the sense of occasion.

01

Wireframe overviewThe page structure separates open-ended discovery from the focused decisions required to reserve a place.

02

Information architecture and navigationCategories, search and event details lead naturally toward ticket selection, checkout and confirmation.

03

UX strategy and design decisionsEditorial browsing creates interest first, while clear dates, venues, pricing and availability support a quick decision.

04

Usability, responsive and accessible foundationsShort booking steps, visible progress, readable hierarchy and keyboard-friendly controls keep the flow usable across screens.

05

Design system and key UX improvementsA consistent grid, type scale and action language replace scattered event information with a clearer path from curiosity to confirmation.

Design Direction

Energetic imagery, disciplined structure.

Bold event photography carries the personality of the site, held together by a calm, consistent grid and type system so the energy never tips into chaos.

Eventora brings discovery and booking together into one coherent experience — one that feels editorial while staying fast and clear at the moments that matter most.
